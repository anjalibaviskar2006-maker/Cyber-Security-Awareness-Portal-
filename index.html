KNOWLEDGE

What is Cyber Security?

🔐

Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, mobile devices, networks, applications and personal information from unauthorized access, cyber attacks and digital threats.

Good cyber security habits help students and internet users safely use online banking, social media, email and other digital services.

BE AWARE

Common Cyber Threats

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites designed to steal passwords and personal information.

🦠

Malware

Harmful software that can damage devices or steal sensitive information.

👤

Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate people into revealing confidential information.

🌐

Fake Websites

Fraudulent websites designed to collect passwords, OTPs or financial information.

🔑

Password Attacks

Attackers try to guess or steal weak passwords to access online accounts.

📱

Mobile Threats

Unsafe applications, links and downloads can put mobile devices at risk.

PROTECT YOURSELF

Online Safety Tips

01

Use Strong Passwords

Use long and unique passwords for different accounts.

02

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Use 2FA whenever it is available on your account.

03

Never Share OTP

Never share OTP, PIN or password with anyone.

04

Check Links Before Clicking

Verify suspicious links before opening them.

05

Update Your Software

Keep your operating system and applications updated.

06

Protect Your Privacy

Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information online.

SOCIAL MEDIA SECURITY

Protect Your Social Media Accounts

📸

Instagram

Keep your account private and enable two-factor authentication.

💬

WhatsApp

Never share verification codes and enable two-step verification.

📧

Email

Avoid suspicious attachments and verify unexpected emails.

👥

Facebook

Review privacy settings and secure your account with 2FA.
